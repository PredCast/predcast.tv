Profile card on hover.

What you see when hovering a username in the live chat, the leaderboard or the live page: level, rank frame, chosen title, XP bar, daily streak and a showcase of up to 3 achievements. No amount is ever shown. Hover a username in the chat below, or reach it with the Tab key.

01 · In context

In the live chat.

A level chip coloured by rank sits before every username. The chips of visible messages load in one batch; the full card loads on hover or focus only. Bets stay anonymous in the chat.

Level chip

Lifetime level in the rank colour (Rookie → Icon). Always shown, even when the player wears a special frame. It replaces the hueForUser colour, which said nothing about the player.

Title, picked by the player

Unlocked by achievements and levels, chosen in the Progression section of the dashboard, together with the frame and the showcase.

Daily streak, no pressure

Days with a check-in: opening the app signed in is enough, no bet needed. A freeze, earned every 7 days (2 max), protects a missed day automatically. No "streak in danger" alert.

Never on the card

No stake, payout or PnL, no Bet Volume achievement (private to its owner), no linked wallets. Gold usernames stay reserved for active subscribers (isFeatured), as today.

New players

A New tag for 14 days. Nothing is sorted or hidden by level: a level 3 reads as clearly as a level 52.
